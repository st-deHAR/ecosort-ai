    The problem

    People often do not know which material an item is made from or how to sort it. Incorrect sorting can contaminate recycling streams.

    📏

    How we measure use

    EcoSort records user-confirmed sorting actions and category feedback locally. These counts measure app activity, not independently verified waste diversion.

    Important limitations

    Image similarity can be wrong, especially with mixed materials, clutter, poor lighting, or multiple objects. Local recycling rules always take priority.
